# Using the console

The hosted console is at [cockpit.prosentriq.com](https://cockpit.prosentriq.com).
Press `Ctrl K` (`⌘ K` on a Mac) anywhere to search pages and actions.

## Monitor

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | What Prosentriq saw, decided and did: open incidents, decisions over the last 7 days, trust metrics (how safely autonomy is working), trends, and the time from sign-up to first finding |
| **Incidents** | Attacks grouped by actor, worst first. Open one for its reasons, ATT&CK techniques and **Storyline**, and to set its status |
| **Approvals & actions** | Actions waiting for a person (level 2, or downgraded by a safety check), and every action taken, with **Undo** |
| **Decision ledger** | Every decision, enforced or not, with its reasons and safety checks. Filter by outcome or indicator |

## Control

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Autonomy dial** | The level, the effective level and what holds it down, circuit breakers and their limits, and the allowlist. See [The Autonomy Dial](../concepts/autonomy.md) |
| **Rules** | Built-in and your own rules, imports, and canary rollouts. See [Rules](rules.md) |
| **Honeytokens** | Plant and revoke decoys. See [Honeytokens](honeytokens.md) |

## Fleet

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Sensors** | Enrollment keys and install commands, every sensor's freshness, version and mode (observe or enforce), per-host autonomy caps, retiring sensors |
| **Integrations** | Destinations, test sends, dead letters, and SOAR. See [Integrations](integrations.md) |

## Govern

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Compliance** | Control status, signed evidence packs, the cyber-insurance pack and the auditor portal. See [Compliance](compliance.md) |
| **Reports** | The Trial Value Report, displacement reports and others |
| **Audit log** | Every privileged action, hash-chained, with a check that the chain is intact |

## Settings

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Members** | Invite people and set roles. See [Teams and access](access.md) |
| **API keys** | Keys for scripts and integrations |
| **My security** | Your two-factor authentication, recovery codes, and deleting your account |
| **Single sign-on** | Your identity provider and verified domains |
| **Organization** | Name, data sharing (Immunity Network), the AI assistant, branding, and deleting the organization |
| **Usage & migration** | Enforcing nodes and cost, the subscription and billing portal, and the migration kit |

**Support** (bottom of the sidebar) holds your support cases and **Data
access**, where an owner lets Prosentriq support see data for a limited time.
See [Getting help](support.md).

**Plans** shows the plans and lets an owner change them. MSPs also have
**Customer fleet**. See [Managed service providers](msp.md).

Your theme (system, light or dark) and links to these docs are in the account
menu.
